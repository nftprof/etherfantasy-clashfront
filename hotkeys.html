⌨ EF MOBA
Keyboard & Mouse Controls
← Launcher

A fast 3-lane MOBA: one hero plus commandable workers (pets), soldiers, and recruited masters. Controls follow the StarCraft / League conventions you already know — right-click to move & fight, QWER for abilities, number keys for control groups. Typing in a chat/name field disables shortcuts automatically.
