## 1. Coordinate frame & scale

- Origin `(0,0)` = **Emberhollow** carnival town. `TOWN.r = 30`.
- The **Wilds** is a disc/square of radius `WILD_R = 150`, displaced by `heightAt()`.
- Hero walking is clamped to a **square** at `±(WILD_R+15) = ±165` (`clampZone`).
- The scenic ground plane is `WILD_R*2+40 = 340` wide → physical edge at `±170`.
- The seven **dungeons/realms** sit far out at `cx,cz = ±520`, reached only by
  **portals** (teleport), never on foot — the Wilds clamp blocks walking there.

```
                      (z = -520)  Feastfall (Ice, T4)
                              |
  Enamora (Earth,T6) --- Valley of Midas (Fire,T3) [x=-520]
        (-520,-520)          |
                             EMBERHOLLOW (0,0)  ---- Mossfang Hollow (Leaf,T1) [x=+520]
                              |                           |
  Isle of Dominus (Lt,T5)     |                  The Abyssal Vault (Mystic,T7) (+520,+520)
        (-520,+520)   Howling Crypt (Phantom,T2) [z=+520]
```
