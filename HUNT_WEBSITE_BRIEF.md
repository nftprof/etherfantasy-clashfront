## 0. One-liner / positioning

**Beyond the Twilight Veil** — a browser-based action-RPG that fuses **Genshin Impact's** open
exploration, elemental combat and aimed-bow feel with **World of Warcraft's** zones, factions,
threat/“con” reading and progression, wrapped around a **deep dark-fantasy isekai** about chasing
love into an Underworld made of desire — where every shadow power you gain costs a piece of your
humanity. **Name your own hero. Nine difficulties, nine endings. Only the bravest reach the truth.**

Genre tags: Action RPG · Dark Fantasy · Isekai · Roguelite-permadeath · Web3 (on-chain currency).

---

## 1. The pitch (Genshin × WoW × deep lore)

- **Genshin-like:** open elemental realms, an **aimed-bow combat** mode (free 360° look, weak-point
  crits), **fog-of-war exploration** with collectibles and hidden vaults, traversal puzzles, a pet
  companion, and a saturated-to-shadowy world that shifts from carnival wonder to underworld dread.
- **WoW-like:** distinct **zones** each themed to a sin/element, **factions** with their own lords and
  motives, a **threat/“con” system** (color-coded danger over every monster), boss mechanics, a
  **journal/codex**, faction **reputation**, and difficulty-gated endgame.
- **Deep lore:** a 7-chapter authored campaign with branching tone (dark/light choices), a betrayal at
  its heart, unlockable **OVA reveals**, and a **9-difficulty ending ladder** that rewards mastery with
  a genuinely different, better ending each tier — culminating in a true "Holy Grail" finale.

## 2. Premise (PUBLIC — safe to market)

The world's lifeblood is **ether**, shaped by desire. Beneath the mortal world lies the **Underworld**
— a realm "sculpted by the very essence of human longing," ruled by the **Desire Weave**, where magic
is a direct extension of your deepest wishes and darkest cravings, and **every spell siphons a sliver
of your own self.** Power has a price.

It begins at a **night carnival.** You and your closest companion are lured through a guarded portal
into the Underworld, where you take up a **living scimitar** that brands you with shadow-sigils — the
first forbidden power, and the Veil's first hook in you. To save the one you love, you must descend
through realms of **Greed, Gluttony, Power and Lust**, gaining darker strength while a fox-masked
stranger tempts you and a shadow-lord, **Kage no Mamoru**, waits in the Castle of Shadows.

The hook: **the love is a trap, the power is a leash, and how much of yourself you keep depends on
how far you're willing to push.**

## 3. ⚠ SPOILERS (internal only — do NOT put on the public site)

- **Ayume (your companion) is the true antagonist.** She found the *Scimitar's Secrets*, made a pact
  with a dark "Whisper," and **orchestrated the entire descent** — she needed a "fated partner," and
  engineered your meeting (the carnival, the portal, the fall). At the climax she takes the onyx
  throne, betrays everyone, and binds the hero to her will.
- **Yui (the fox-masked tempter)** is the red herring — suspected as the villain, she is actually the
  **tragic, sacrificed** one. She is **only redeemed in the difficulty-9 "Holy Grail" ending.**
- **The final boss is Ayume herself** (after Kage no Mamoru), in a multi-phase fight.
- The OVAs (unlocked at a difficulty-6 clear) reveal her origin and the throne betrayal as "found
  footage" reels.

## 4. Characters

- **The Hero (player-named; canon "Kai / Jiro"):** an ordinary young person drawn into the Veil; gains
  shadow power at the cost of humanity. The only playable character. *(Player types their own name at
  start; the game and dialogue use it throughout.)*
- **Ayume:** your devoted companion and love interest. *(Public: the heart of the story. Internal: the
  architect — see spoilers.)*
- **Yui:** a captivating, fox-masked, provocative stranger with a hidden agenda — temptress and enigma.
- **Barkindle:** your loyal fire-touched pet companion, with you from the very first step.
- **Kage no Mamoru:** the shadow-lord of the Castle of Shadows (Yomi no Tō); penultimate threat.
- **The Fortune Teller / Yume / Whisper:** an oracle who foretells your doom, a wise guide who arms you,
  and the seductive dark voice that drives the corruption.

## 5. The world (zones & factions)

- **Emberhollow Carnival (hub):** the safe, vibrant night carnival — stalls, masquerade grove, fortune
  tent, the altar and the portal. The real-world "easy" side; the doorway to the Underworld.
- **The Sin-Realms** (each its own zone + faction lord, escalating danger):
  - **Valley of Midas — Greed:** streets of gold, opulence over a spiritless waste. Lord: **Aurelian the Gilded King.**
  - **Feastfall Plains — Gluttony:** an endless banquet and storms of unquenchable hunger. Lord: **Throngullet the Endless Maw.**
  - **Isle of Dominus — Power:** floating fortresses and arenas, static-charged air, endless power-plays. Lord: **Vael the Crowned Tyrant.**
  - **Enamora — Lust:** a perpetual-twilight garden of seduction. Lord: **Seraphel the Beguiling Rose.**
- **The Castle of Shadows (Abyssal Vault):** Kage no Mamoru's seat — the finale.
- **Factions:** each realm has a faction with its own broken logic; the player earns **reputation** and a
  **bestiary/codex** as they cleanse each realm.

## 6. Systems & features (the "depth")

- **Name-your-hero + visual-novel dialogue** (portrait + typewriter + voice) drives a fully authored,
  first-person 7-chapter campaign.
- **Desire-Weave combat:** element-based skills (arm-then-aim cast), an **aimed-bow mode** (Genshin-style
  free look + weak-point crits), monster **personality AI**, and per-boss signature mechanics.
- **Humanity ⇄ Corruption gauge:** shadow power makes you stronger but fills corruption; cross the
  **Critical Limit** and you're consumed (a themed permadeath). Anchors/human food pull you back. This
  is the moral + mechanical spine.
- **Threat “con” markers:** every monster shows a color-coded danger badge (grey→green→white→orange→red→
  purple "???" = flee), WoW-style.
- **Exploration:** **fog-of-war** map, generous road pickups, **rare caches** in hard-to-reach corners,
  **traversal** (a Flash/blink to cross gaps), and **hidden vaults behind puzzles**.
- **Collectibles & codex:** **Memory Shards** unlock lore + OVA fragments; a faction bestiary.
- **9-difficulty → 9-ending ladder** (see §7) — the replay engine.
- **Permadeath with a second chance:** die and you can pay to revive once (injured, 80% power, for 24h);
  die again in that window and it's permanent — gear lost, but progress/skills kept.
- **Co-op (planned):** share a session code; a second player joins as Barkindle, Yui, or Ayume.
- **Pet companion (Barkindle):** fights beside you, gatherable, commandable (MOBA-style), revivable.

## 7. The replay hook — difficulty → ending ladder

The 9 difficulties aren't just harder numbers — **each cleared tier unlocks a different, better ending.**
The higher you climb, the more of yourself and your friends you save:

- **Diff 1–5:** escalating tragic endings (Consumed → The Pawn → Fractured → The Witness → Defiance).
- **Diff 6 — "The Truth":** you recover the memories → **the OVA reveals unlock.**
- **Diff 7–8:** hopeful endings (Embers of Hope → Two Saved).
- **Diff 9 — "THE HOLY GRAIL":** the one true happy ending — corruption purged, the tragic character
  redeemed, everyone walks out of the Veil together. **Earned only at the very top.**

This gives the game enormous replay depth: you play to *change the ending.*

## 8. Web3 / on-chain angle

- The in-game currency **💎 CT (Carat)** is an **on-chain token** (Pentagon Games). It's earned by
  playing, shared across game modes, and is being designed to be **freezable / anti-cheat-enforced on
  chain** (with a login system + server-side validation as the backend lands). Client-side anti-cheat
  exists today as a deterrent.
- Marketing angle: a **playable, story-rich web game** where progression ties to a real on-chain asset —
  "play the story, earn the Carat."

---

## 9. Tone & style

Oscillates between the **saturated wonder of the carnival** and the **stark, shadowy underworld** —
romance and dread, beauty and corruption. Themes: **destiny vs. free will, the allure of forbidden
power, and the strength (and danger) of love.** Think *Made in Abyss / Berserk*-adjacent darkness with
a Genshin sheen. Graphics are stylized/low-poly 3D (browser, Three.js) — **the pitch is depth of story
and systems, not photorealism.**
