What you do here

Hold land

Own or rent parcels. Enrich them, garrison them, build defense modules on the seeded slots. A parcel with a Master and troops on it is yours to defend; a parcel you march into is contested.

March

Route across the parcel graph. Marches take real time. Two commuters can pass with EVASIVE stance; a HOSTILE march ends in a fight. Owned foreign land is a blockade — you can attack it as a destination, never walk through it.

Fight — or don't

Command mode is the default: watch the battle from above, issue orders, save your live-battle slot. Hero mode drops you inside the 3D engine as your Master. Both play the same match. Retreat, FLEE, or push through — the loser retreats through the edge they came from; if that edge is occupied, they're pincered.

Weather changes days

Rain, storm, fog, snow, heatwave, wind — rolled per continent per day, deterministic per world seed. Water pets get +15% in rain. Fire pets take −15%. Terrain stacks. Total swing capped at ±35%.

The world remembers

Great battles auto-name themselves after the land they happened on and archive to a public Chronicle. First-through-a-gate, first boss-kill, first town-founder — inscribed at the site in your name, forever.

NFTs mean something

Land NFTs = the parcels you play on (Ethereum L2, Polygon L3). Master NFTs = the champions you lead. Pet NFTs = summon-rights: own the DNA, summon that species anywhere (at a premium). More on the NFT page.
