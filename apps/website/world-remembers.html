The World Remembers

Your fights are the world's history

A big enough battle isn't just a stat — the world names it after the ground it happened on, archives it to a public feed, and drops a monument at the site. First deeds are inscribed there in your name, permanently.

Auto-named battles

Battles above a casualty threshold ⚙ pick their own name from the parcel they happened on: "The Second Siege of Rustbriar," "The Fog-Line at Long Reach," "Choco's Retreat," "Broken Watch." Naming is deterministic — same casualty count + same parcel + same seed = same name every time, so a replay reads the same as the live account.

The World Chronicle

A public, always-on feed of the named battles. Anyone can read it. Filter by continent, by governor, by Master, by outcome. It's the game's history book — and because it's fed by real battles that actually happened, it's real.

Chronicle entries carry the participant list, casualty counts, winning + losing Masters, and (with fog restrictions) the winning strategy summary. Great victories highlight; ties + defeats appear too — every named battle counts.

Monuments on the map

Named-battle sites drop a monument POI at the parcel — visible on the overworld forever. Graveyards seed Phantom pets over time — the dead don't leave. Battlefields where a boss fell mark their kill point. Sanctuaries where a hero saved a rout are visible from adjacent parcels.

Monuments don't fade. They accumulate. Old wars stay visible to new players — the ground carries its history.

First deeds — inscribed in your name

Some events happen ONCE in the world's lifetime. When they happen, they inscribe at the site — in the player's name — permanently.

  • First through each gate — the great gates of the world (Hell Shaft, Vault-Gate, Sky Throne approach, Diminishing Stair)
  • First through each port — Porthaven, Aeropolis, HS1 landing
  • First boss-kill — every wild boss's slayer is inscribed at the fall site
  • First town-founder — the first player to establish an owned town at a valid site
  • First EPIC holder — the first player to own an EPIC-tier estate on each continent
  • First to complete the Descent rites — Midway Potion → Last Fortune → Diminishing Stair → Blood Scimitar → Homeward Bowl

These aren't stats on a leaderboard. They're inscriptions IN the world, at the site of the deed, forever. Every player who visits will see the name.

Named artifacts — Singulars

36 one-of-one items exist in the world's data. Legendary weapons, rites, potions — each in a specific place with a specific story. Some are permanent (bind to first-finder in the Chronicle). Some are consumables tied to the Descent rites.

The Midway Potion. The Last Fortune. The Blood Scimitar. The Homeward Bowl. Named weapons bound to art from the MOBA hero-gear catalog. Each Singular is somewhere. Each will only be found once.
