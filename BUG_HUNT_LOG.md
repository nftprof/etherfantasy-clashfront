2026-07-01T01:20Z — [static] Master "heals under fire" regen bug. DETECTED: static read of soldierAI's recruited-master follow block (index.html ~3342) + dmgUnit (~2533). ROOT CAUSE: the master's out-of-combat regen gate uses `u._cbtT`, but `_cbtT` was stamped ONLY by the master's own foe-scan inside soldierAI — dmgUnit never stamped it (it stamps `cbtT` for heroes/bosses/structures, not soldier-masters). So a recruited master damaged from beyond its ~22u detection radius (enemy tower range 21, long-range projectiles/abilities) regenerated 5%/s WHILE being shot; conversely, the flee/fight branches self-stamped `_cbtT` every frame, so a master that had disengaged refused to heal as long as any foe stayed in view. FIX (surgical, 2 spots): (1) dmgUnit now stamps `if(u.sub==='master'&&amt>0)u._cbtT=gameT;` on damage, mirroring the adjacent hero/boss/structure `cbtT` line; (2) removed the two per-frame `u._cbtT=gameT` self-stamps in the master follow-AI branches so regen keys off REAL damage taken (5s out-of-combat). VALIDATION: outputs/master_regen_check_op48.js — `node --check` passed; 3 assertions pass — A) no heal while taking damage with foe undetected (was the bug), B) heals 5s after last hit even if a foe lingers in view (flee actually recovers now), C) no heal while trading melee blows. No new per-frame allocations; desktop+mobile unaffected; client gameplay lane only.

2026-07-01T07:22Z — [static] Player MELEE hero freezes on auto-attack near a flyer (target-acquisition gap). DETECTED: static read of the two HAND-ROLLED player-hero target scans — amScan (index.html ~2950, used by attack-move) and the idle auto-attack loop (~5244) — cross-checked against the canonical nearestEnemy (~2713). ROOT CAUSE: nearestEnemy correctly skips air units a unit can't reach (`if(v.air&&noAir)continue;`, noAir=!canHitAir), but the two hero scans did NOT replicate that gate — they picked the nearest enemy of any kind. So when the closest enemy to a MELEE hero (range≤4 → canHitAir false) was an AIR flyer (u.air set for non-hero units with flyer types / MODEL_CAL.AIR, hovering at y=3.2), the scan locked onto it; tryAttack's air guard (l.2735 `if(t.air&&!canHitAir(u)){u.target=null;return false;}`) then instantly nulled the target and returned false, so the hero neither attacked nor moved — it STALLED in place every frame, re-picking the same unhittable flyer, ignoring a reachable ground creep right behind it. FIX (surgical, 2 spots, same root cause): added `if(v.air&&!canHitAir(h))continue;` to amScan and `if(v.air&&!canHitAir(pHero))continue;` to the idle loop, mirroring nearestEnemy's gate. A melee hero now skips flyers it can't hit and engages the nearest GROUND enemy; ranged heroes (canHitAir true) are unaffected and still target air. VALIDATION: outputs/hero_air_target_check_op48.js — `node --check` passed; 8 assertions pass — A) old scan picks the nearer air unit → melee hero stalls (bug reproduced); B) fixed scan skips the flyer, picks the ground creep, hero can attack; C) ranged hero still targets air (no regression); D) melee hero with ONLY air enemies returns null (no phantom lock/freeze); E) with no air units old==new (zero behaviour change). No new per-frame allocations (one boolean test per candidate, reuses existing canHitAir); desktop+mobile unaffected; client gameplay lane only; reversible (remove the two added clauses).
