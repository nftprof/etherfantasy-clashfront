FAQ

Good questions.

What's the difference between the World Atlas and the Designer?

The World Atlas is the map of the whole world — use it to see where everything is. The Designer is a workshop for a single parcel — use it to decide what one piece of land looks like when it's fought over. The Atlas shows you the world; the Designer lets you shape your part of it.

Do I need land to play Clash Front?

No. Anyone can play. Owning land means a specific piece of the world is yours: you decide what its battlefield looks like, and it carries your name.

Is claiming free?

If you hold a LAND size token, claiming the matching plot is a free mint — you only pay the network gas fee for the two transactions (approve, then claim).

Which blockchains?

Estates (Small to Epic) are on Ethereum. Single parcels are on Polygon. You'll need a wallet such as MetaMask connected to the right network.

Can I change my land's design later?

Yes. Every design is saved with a version history. Freeze a design to lock its look; generate a new one whenever you want to change it. Every design must pass the same fairness and playability checks.

What does "fair and playable" mean?

Every map is checked automatically before it's saved — both sides can reach each other, no spawn is walled off, castle gates are wide enough, and no layout gives one side an unfair edge. A design that fails isn't saved.

Can I look around without signing in?

Yes. The World Atlas, the Designer and the 3D view are open to everyone. Signing in (with a Pentagon Games account or a wallet) is only needed to design or claim land you own.
