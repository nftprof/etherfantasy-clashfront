**no monsters — only friendly pets**, wonder & character establishment) → cross **THE RIFT** and
claim the **BLOOD SCIMITAR** (a pact with a price, tied to the Corruption gauge) → **descend into
the UNDERWORLD, which is ONE-WAY for a while** (trapped, hunted by scent/food) → mid/late **find the
way BACK** to the real world → then **FREE TWO-WAY travel** between worlds (the world finally opens)
→ **THEN the OVA** (OVAs unlock at diff-6 clear; Holy Grail / Yui redeemed at diff-9). The world map
opens **incrementally** (FF II style); keep the early story on guided rails.
