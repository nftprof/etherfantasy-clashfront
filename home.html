💎 CT (Carat) — the shared currency

CT ties the two games together. You start with 2. Earn it by surviving in EF Hunt; spend it staking MOBA tournaments and buying permanent Hunt gear. It lives in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded.

How it flows: EF Hunt pays CT at checkpoints (1h / 2.5h / map-clear). Difficulty 1 pays ~3 CT for a full run; difficulty 9 ("hell") pays ~30. In the MOBA, hosts can stake a tournament for a 1 CT entry; winners split the pot. Saving CT makes you richer; spending it reaches deeper difficulties and bigger stakes.

🚪 Getting started & win condition ›

Launch start_game.bat (serves at http://localhost:8000 so 3D models + roster load), open the game, enter a summoner name on the login screen, and press Enter the Rift. Your name and progression (including mastery) save to your browser.

  • Win: destroy the enemy core.
  • Lose: only when all your bases fall — your hero dying is just a respawn timer.
  • Match length: tuned for 10–20 minutes. Minion waves grow +8%/min; at 15:00 OVERTIME structures take +35% damage so stalemates break.
🐲 Champions & Mastery ›

Every champion is a real monster from the roster with one or two elemental types; the primary type decides its Q/W/E kit. Champions belong to evolution chains, so a hero can evolve into stronger forms mid-match. The current playable heroes are Irene (archer, ranged), Kai (swordsman, melee) and Leah (mage, ranged + staff melee).

★ Hero Mastery grows with practice like a personal rune page:

  • +1 point / 15s played, plus +10 win / +4 loss per match.
  • Capped curve to Lv 10 (1,000 pts). Each level = +0.5% HP & attack, hard-capped at +5% — flavor, never pay-to-grind-to-win.
  • Shown on every champion card, saved in your browser.

✨ Skills & archetypes ›

Modern aim-then-cast targeting: press the key, see a live preview (ring for area, line for direction), click to cast. Clicking near the enemy hero snaps the cast onto them. Each of the 17 elements maps to a kit from six archetypes:

💰 Economy — gold & wood ›

Two resources: gold and wood. Your hero earns gold from kills; peons (pet monsters) gather both, StarCraft-style — select a peon, right-click a tree or gold vein, and it hauls loads to the nearest base on a loop.

  • Kills can drop eggs — walk a hero or pet over one to hatch a free peon.
  • Gold gates the Item Shop, unit training, buildings and evolution.
  • Caps: 32 peons, 32 soldiers.
🏰 Building & units ›

Glowing rings are build slots: build a Tower (auto-attacks) or an Expansion Base (up to 3 total; near-gold slots are guarded by wild camps you must clear). Click a base/town hall for its menu — upgrade defense, train peons/footmen/archers, and evolve your hero.

🌪 Type chart, combat & HUD ›

Elemental effectiveness on every hit: ×1.5 strong, ×0.7 resisted (orange numbers = super effective, grey = resisted, white = normal). 17 types: Fire, Water, Leaf, Lightning, Earth, Ice, Combat, Toxin, Telepath, Insect, Rock, Phantom, Dragon, Iron, Flyer, Mystic, Neutral. The full chart prints on champion select.

  • Fountain: heroes spawn/respawn behind their town hall and heal fast (14% HP/s). T recalls home.
  • Town-hall healing: near any friendly base regen 70 HP + 25 mana/s.
  • Map paths: rock ridges seal the border and split the jungle — no sneaking around towers along the edge (towers: 85 dmg, range 21).
  • Respawn: 6s + 2s/level, shown on your portrait.
  • HUD: portrait + HP/mana + Q/W/E/F buttons (bottom); clock, base count, core HP (top); minimap with lanes, boss rings, dropped legendaries (right).
🌐 Multiplayer & tournaments (up to 2v2) ›

On champion select pick ⚡ Quick Match (auto-join/host), Host Room (4-letter code) or Join Room. Players can also join mid-game, taking over the AI hero or dropping in as a 3rd/4th hero — choosing 🤝 Co-op, ⚔ Versus or 🎲 Any.

  • Same network: friends open http://<host-ip>:8000 in their browser.
  • Signaling: free PeerJS cloud by default; run start_peer_server.bat for fully-local LAN.
  • Economy online: each hero has their own gold purse; bases/armies shared per side.
  • Disconnects: AI retakes a hero whose player leaves.
  • 🏆 Tournaments: stake a match — entry 1 💎CT; winners get entry back + 50% of losing/forfeited fees, split. Dropping out forfeits your fee.

🌄 Overview & the 4-hour arc ›

From Emberhollow you head into sculpted wilds — high crags, glowing fens — through three dungeons to a final boss at the 4-hour mark. Survive scaling waves, mini-bosses every 30 minutes, and Blood Moons. Clear the map to push your difficulty up by +1 (max 9).

  • One run per 8 hours — pacing is deliberate.
  • Dev test mode: open pve.html?fast=1 for an accelerated run.
🏹 Hunters (3 daily-rotating) ›

Pick from 3 hunters that rotate daily. Each plays like its MOBA counterpart — same model, animation, voice and skill feel — so a champion you know in the arena handles the same in the wilds. Hunters keep their skills and CT through death; everything else is at risk.

🐾 Taming & pets ›

Hatchlings emerge from eggs; tame them to gain a pet that fights alongside you and barks its own name. The taming skill line raises success odds and pet strength. Pets persist within a run and add to your damage and survivability.

🌲 Gathering & building ›

Harvest wood and gold to build towers, walls and heal totems that hold the line between waves. The harvest skill line speeds gathering. Smart base placement around chokepoints is the difference between surviving a Blood Moon and wiping.

🎯 Weak-spot sniping & boons ›
  • Hunter's Overlooks: perch on vantage points to land weak-spot shots for bonus damage on big targets.
  • Boon builds: pick run-defining boons that stack into a build, roguelite-style.
  • 🩸 Blood Moons: periodic surges — denser, tougher waves with richer rewards.
☠ Difficulty, CT earnings & hardcore ›
  • CT checkpoints at 1h / 2.5h / map-clear (20 / 30 / 50% of the difficulty payout). Diff 1 ≈ 3 CT for the full run; diff 9 ("hell") ≈ 30.
  • Permanent gear: 6 upgrade lines (attack, HP, speed, taming, harvest, pet damage), 3 tiers each, bought with CT. Gear carries between runs — but monsters scale to your gear score, so power has a price.
  • Hardcore: die without a 🧪 revive potion (2 CT) and you lose ALL gear and difficulty progress — only your CT balance survives.
  • Clear the map → difficulty +1 (max 9).

📱 Mobile & touch mode

Both games auto-detect phones and tablets and switch to a touch layout — no setting to flip. A desktop with a mouse stays on the classic keyboard/mouse controls. You can also force it with ?touch=1 in the URL.

🕹️

Left joystick

Hold and drag the left thumb pad to move your hero in any direction — release to stop. Replaces right-click-to-move.

🔘

Ability buttons

Right-side buttons for Q / W / E / R (super) and F (pet heal). Tap to aim, tap the field to cast; aimed skills show the same preview as desktop.

👆

Tap to target

Tap an enemy to attack-move toward it; tap the ground to walk there. Tap the minimap to pan the camera.

🤏

Pinch & drag

Pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan. The HUD scales up so buttons stay thumb-sized on small screens.

Tip: add the page to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience, and turn the phone landscape for the most play area. Performance scales down model detail automatically on lower-end devices to keep loading fast.

❓ FAQ

Do I need to install anything? ›

No build step or app store — just run the local server (start_game.bat) and open it in a browser. Models and the roster load from http://localhost:8000.

Is my progress saved? Where? ›

Yes — summoner name, hero mastery, CT wallet and Hunt gear all live in your browser's localStorage on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Can my friends play with me? ›

In the MOBA, yes — up to 2v2 via Quick Match / room codes. On the same network they just open http://<your-ip>:8000. EF Hunt is single-player PVE.

What happens when I die? ›

MOBA: a respawn timer, nothing lost (except a carried legendary, which drops). Hunt: hardcore — without a revive potion you lose all gear and difficulty progress, keeping only CT and skills.

How do the two games connect? ›

They share the monster roster, the hero models/animation/voice, and the 💎 CT wallet. Earn CT hunting, spend it staking MOBA tournaments and buying permanent Hunt gear.
