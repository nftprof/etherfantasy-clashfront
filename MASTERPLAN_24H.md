# BEYOND THE TWILIGHT VEIL — Master Plan & 24-Hour Autonomous Build Backlog

> The full-scale design + the work queue for the next 24 hours. Goal: a story-first ARPG with
> **Genshin-level depth and narrative** (not graphics) built on `pve.html`. An automated builder
> ("twilight-veil-cron") works this backlog **every 20 minutes for 24 hours**, shipping one
> validated feature per run, **60% of effort on the main narrative + playable path to the end**,
> 40% on world/exploration depth. This doc is the single source of truth the cron reads each run.

---

## 1. North star

A player names their hero, is pulled with **Ayume** (and their pet **Barkindle**) from a carnival
into the Underworld, gains shadow power at the cost of **Humanity/Corruption**, is joined and tempted
by **Yui**, and chases a love that turns out to be a trap — **Ayume orchestrated everything.** The
9-difficulty grind is not filler: **each cleared difficulty unlocks a better ending**, the OVAs (the
truth) unlock at **difficulty 6**, and the **Holy Grail ending at difficulty 9** is the only one where
**Yui is redeemed and becomes good.** Always something to find on the road (Genshin-style pickups),
a fog-of-war map with traversal-gated secrets, and a complete, authored campaign with real stakes.

---

## 2. The difficulty → ending ladder (the spine of replay; user-specified)

Beating the final confrontation shows an **ending cutscene chosen by the difficulty you cleared it
at**. Higher difficulty = you endured more = you keep more of yourself and your friends. Persisted in
`SAVE.endingsSeen` + `SAVE.bestEnding`; OVA unlock in `SAVE.ovaUnlocked`.

| Diff cleared | Ending | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **"Consumed"** | Corruption takes you mid-fight; you become a shade of the Veil. The bleakest. |
| **2** | **"The Pawn"** | Ayume takes the onyx throne; you're bound to her will; Yui is absorbed. The canonical tragedy. |
| **3** | **"Fractured"** | Same fall, but you resist a moment — a flicker of self remains. A little more lore shown. |
| **4** | **"The Witness"** | You survive lucid; you *see* Ayume's true face but cannot act. More of the scheme revealed. |
| **5** | **"Defiance"** | You break the blood-binding and flee the castle — alive, corrupted, alone. Yui still lost. |
| **6** | **"The Truth"** → **OVA UNLOCKS** | You escape *and* recover the memories — OVA1 (Ayume's origin) & OVA2 (the betrayal) become playable from the title. |
| **7** | **"Embers of Hope"** | You return for Yui; partial rescue — she's freed but changed. |
| **8** | **"Two Saved"** | You resist the Critical Limit enough to pull both Yui *and* the real Ayume back from the dark. |
| **9** | **"HOLY GRAIL"** | The true ending: purge the corruption, **Yui turns good** and is redeemed; you, a freed Ayume, and a good Yui leave the Veil together. Earned only at the top. |

- **Gating:** OVAs are locked until a **diff-6 clear**; the Holy Grail epilogue only plays on a **diff-9
  clear**. Lower clears show their tier ending and bump difficulty as today.
- **Delivery:** endings & OVAs are **dialogue/cutscene sequences** (reuse the `playDialogue` system +
  the found-footage "grainy" styling). Cheap to build, huge payoff.
- **Title "▶ Memories" button** appears once `ovaUnlocked` — replays OVA1/OVA2 (+ Holy Grail epilogue
  if diff-9 cleared).

## 3. Main narrative — the playable path to the end (60% of effort)

Seven episodes (see `PIVOT_TWILIGHT_VEIL.md` §6) as linear stages over the existing zones, each with:
intro dialogue → party stance (with/against) → signature beat (mostly converted to battles) → outro.
`SAVE.episode` gates progression; the Carnival is the hub. After Episode 7's final boss → the **ending
ladder** (§2). Build front-to-back so every run leaves the campaign **playable to its current end**.

Narrative build priorities (the cron favours these — 60%):
1. Episode intro/outro **dialogue scenes** (Ep1 done as the launch intro; do Ep2→7).
2. **Episode gating + a chapter-select** screen (replaces/fronts the zone map).
3. **Corruption/Humanity gauge** (merges injury/permadeath/Blight — see pivot §4) + shadow-skill unlocks.
4. **Companions** Ayume/Yui as join-able allies that flip to enemies at the betrayal (Ep7).
5. The **final boss → ending ladder → OVA** chain (§2).
6. Carnival hub re-skin + the Fortune Teller oracle; realm re-skins (Greed/Gluttony/Power/Lust).
7. Per-episode signature beats as battles (bottle-smash tutorial, mask hunt, sigil-trace, blooms…).

## 4. Exploration & world depth (40% of effort — the "Genshin" layer)

- **Fog-of-war minimap:** the `#mm` minimap (and `M` world map) start dark; reveal cells as you visit
  (`SAVE`/run-scoped visited grid). Unexplored = fogged. Encourages roaming.
- **Always something on the road:** scatter **pickups** generously — gold, mats, gear, and rare
  **weapon/armor caches** — especially in **far, hard-to-reach corners**. A faint glint/marker hints.
- **Hard-to-reach + traversal gates:** put the best caches behind **map edges/gaps** and ledges.
  **Flash/Blink skill = traversal**: once you have Flash you can **JUMP across gaps** to otherwise
  unreachable areas (mirrors the MOBA's move-speed upgrade idea, but as a *traversal unlock*). Give
  the map **real edges** (bounded), with islands/ledges only Flash can reach.
- **Puzzles to open the map (AFK-Arena style):** light gating puzzles — lever/sigil sequences,
  pressure-plate orders, lantern-lighting, color-locks (reuse the bloom puzzle) — that open shortcuts,
  vaults, or new regions. One signature puzzle per realm.
- **Collectibles with payoff:** "memory shards" scattered in the world that unlock lore/OVA fragments;
  a collection screen. Ties exploration to the narrative.
- **Vistas/waypoints:** unlocking a waypoint reveals nearby map + grants a small reward (Genshin's
  statue/teleport feel) — extend the existing waypoint system.

## 6. AUTONOMOUS BUILD PROTOCOL (read every cron run, then act)

Each run, the builder must:

1. **Read** this file + `PIVOT_TWILIGHT_VEIL.md` + `AGENTS_PVE.md` §0 (Definition of Done) + the
   tail of `BUILD_LOG.md` (what was done last) to avoid repeats and pick up context.
2. **Pick the next unchecked item — P0 PLAYTEST FIXES FIRST, then NARRATIVE.** If any item in the §7
   "P0 — PLAYTEST FIXES" block is unchecked, do the topmost one (these are live user-reported issues —
   highest priority of all). Only when all P0 items are ticked do you proceed to the N-items. Then:
   The single goal is that a player can sit down
   and **play through the COMPLETE story, first-person, start to finish**: name themselves → carnival
   intro → all 7 episodes (each with its dialogue scenes, party stance, and a battle/beat) → the
   betrayal → final boss → the ending ladder → OVA/Holy Grail. So: **work the N-items in §7 strictly
   in order until EVERY N-item is ticked** and the campaign is playable end-to-end. Do **not** start
   the W (exploration) items until the story is complete and playable. First-person means the writing
   is personal — second person / `[NAME]`, the player IS Kai, scenes address them directly.
3. **Implement ONE self-contained feature** in `pve.html` (or a sibling file). Keep it small enough
   to finish + validate in the run.
4. **Validate statically** (the sandbox can't run a browser and freezes large-file tails):
   - Write each new code block to a uniquely-named scratch `.js` and `node --check` it.
   - Extract the head region (`<script>`+1 .. last bash-visible line) and `node --check` — fix any
     real error before finishing (this method has caught real bugs; trust it).
   - Use the Read/Edit tools for the true file; only trust bash for the head region.
5. **Apply Definition of Done:** update the in-game 📖 guide / `wiki.html` for any player-facing
   change, and append a dated bullet to `AGENTS_PVE.md`'s latest session section.
6. **Append a one-line entry to `BUILD_LOG.md`** (date, item, files touched, validation result) and
   **tick the backlog item here**.
7. **Stop after 24h:** if the current local time is past **{DEADLINE}**, do nothing and note in
   `BUILD_LOG.md` that the window has closed (the schedule can then be disabled).

Guardrails: never delete working systems; keep CT/anti-cheat intact; small reversible edits;
when unsure between two items, choose the one advancing the **main narrative to its end**.
