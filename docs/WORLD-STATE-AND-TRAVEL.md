# The Shape of the World — travel between the surface, the sky, and the deep

> **Website world-state description (player-facing).** Canon-accurate to the routes the
> world map draws today (`apps/server/public/js/world.js`, registry `zoneLinks`,
> `docs/maps/OVERWORLD-CONNECTIONS.md §3`). Lore backing: `docs/lore/WORLD-CHRONICLE.md`.
> Twelve zones on three tiers, plus two prestige isles reachable by no road at all.

---

## The world has three levels

The world was not built — it was **wanted** into being, and longing has weight. The heavy
wants sank and carved the **underworld**; the light wants rose and crystallized into the
**sky**; between them lies the **surface**, where wanting is still a choice. So the map reads
as three tiers stacked in the dark:

| Tier | Zones | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **☁ The Sky (High Society)** | Aeropolis · Emberfall · Empyrea | Frozen aspiration — floating isles; *ascent must be earned* |
| **▦ The Surface** | Tianxia · Mythoria · Porthaven · Arcadia | The mortal world — four continents, a sea between them |
| **▼ The Underworld** | Ironhold · Blackmere · Luxuria | Desire pooled downward — caverns *wanted* into the stone |
| **✦ Prestige isles** | Olympus · Fortuna | Reachable by **no route** — teleport / invitation only |

Each tier down (or up) is **stronger and stranger** than the last — the frontier bands rise
from ~×1 on the home coasts to **×5** in the Inferno Vault. You do not wander there; you fight
your way, tier by tier, and the world tests you at every gate.

---

## The surface — a sea between four shores

The four continents ring a central sea. Ships run the coastal lanes:

```
Mythoria ⇄ Porthaven ⇄ Tianxia ⇄ Arcadia ⇄ Mythoria     (sea lanes)
```

- **Mythoria, Porthaven, Arcadia** are the **starting shores** — where new warlords begin.
- **Tianxia** sits at the world's centre of gravity, *"all under heaven — all roads lead here."*
  It is **not** a starting zone; you advance to it. The great **Shaft** pierces its heart — the
  wound where the deep first broke through, and the army road down.

Crossing the sea to another continent is a **cross-server voyage** (each continent is its own
regional server — Singapore, Tokyo, US West, Montréal). You reserve passage at a dock and sail;
your warband travels with you.

---

## ▼ The descent — a straight road down (the Underworld)

The underworld is a **linear descent**. There is one way down for an army, and it goes one
level at a time:

```
Tianxia ──(the Shaft)──▶ Ironhold ──(gate)──▶ Blackmere ──(gate)──▶ Luxuria
  surface                  UW1                   UW2                  UW3
                                                                   (deepest)
```

- **The Shaft** (Tianxia → Ironhold) is the loud, open road — the way an **army** goes down.
- Between each depth stands a **sealed boss-gate** (the locks of an ancient binding). You do
  not simply walk through; the gate-warden must be beaten, over and over, and each is stronger
  than any mortal host (×2.5 → ×3.5 → **×5.0**).
- **The Diminishing Stair** — a second, secret way down. From **Carnavale** (Mythoria's
  south-west carnival coast) a portal drops straight to **Blackmere**, skipping the Shaft. But
  the Stair is **single-file, souls only — no armies**: you go down alone, growing smaller with
  every step, to make your bargain. (It is a shortcut for a lone Master, never for a host.)

**The Diminution.** The deep does not grow to swallow you — *it diminishes you*. In Blackmere
every soul stands at two-thirds its true height; in Luxuria, a **sixth** — the Gardens seem a
country, the Vault a mountain. Nothing there is actually large. **You are small.** (Stats and
geometry never change — only how vast the world *reads*.)

---

## ☁ The ascent — a gate, then a fork (the Sky)

The sky is the **mirror** of the descent, but a deliberately **different shape**. Where the
deep is a straight road, the heights **branch** — because the heights are **at war**.

```
Arcadia ──▶ Aeropolis ──▶ Emberfall      (Emberfall and Empyrea
Porthaven ─▶  (HS1)    └─▶ Empyrea         are NOT connected —
              gateway                       the War of the Sky Throne)
```

- **Aeropolis is the Gate to Heaven.** *Every* surface airship lands here first — from Arcadia
  and from Porthaven both. There is no shortcut past it: **nothing reaches the upper sky without
  passing the gateway.** *"The first mooring above the clouds; ascent is earned here or nowhere."*
- From the gateway you **choose your climb — Emberfall OR Empyrea, one or the other.** You pick
  your side at the gate.
- **There is no lane between Emberfall and Empyrea.** The two upper isles are severed by the
  **War of the Sky Throne**: **Emberfall** — the *fallen angels*, corruption risen out of the
  deep to besiege the height — makes war on **Empyrea**, the pinnacle. To reach the other isle
  you must come **back down to the gateway** and climb the far branch.

So the loop closes: the corruption that **sank** into the deep now **rises** to storm the top,
and the map itself carries the story — a straight road down, a war-split fork up.

---

## ✦ The prestige isles — no road at all

**Olympus** (the founders) and **Fortuna** (the brightest voices) rise in the bay and connect to
**nothing** — no sea lane, no airship, no gate. They were never meant to be *marched upon*;
arrival is by **teleport / invitation** only. On the map they float apart, unreachable by any
line.

---

## How this shapes travel, right now

Every leg on the map is one of five kinds of passage — and each has its own rules:

| Passage | Where | Who / how |
|---|---|---|
| **⚓ Sea lane** | between the four surface continents | ships; a **cross-server voyage** (dock reserve + passage fee) |
| **☁ Airship lane** | surface → Aeropolis, and Aeropolis → Emberfall / Empyrea | airships; the sky is gated behind the Aeropolis gateway |
| **▼ The Shaft** | Tianxia → Ironhold | the army road down — the open descent |
| **⛬ Boss-gate** | Ironhold → Blackmere → Luxuria | sealed; the warden must be broken to pass deeper |
| **✦ The Stair (portal)** | Carnavale → Blackmere | single-file, **souls only, no armies** |

Practical consequences today:

1. **You cannot skip a tier.** Sky and deep are reached only through their gateways (Aeropolis
   above, the Shaft/Stair below). No surface→upper-sky and no surface→deep-cavern shortcut.
2. **The gateway is a chokepoint.** Aeropolis (up) and the boss-gates (down) are where advances
   stall and wars are fought — hold the gate, hold the tier.
3. **Picking a sky side is a commitment.** Because Emberfall and Empyrea aren't linked, choosing
   one at the gateway means a full return trip to switch — the war makes the sky a fork, not a loop.
4. **Crossing continents crosses servers.** Each continent is a regional shard; a voyage is an
   inter-shard handoff, and travel carries fees (dock reserve + passage) that feed the world's
   economy (a share burns — the net-sink).
5. **The deeper you go, the stronger the world and the smaller you feel** — the frontier bands
   (×1 → ×5) and the Diminution both bite hardest at the bottom.

*The underworld is a ladder; the sky is a crossroads at war. Same idea — gated tiers you earn
your way through — deliberately opposite shapes.*
