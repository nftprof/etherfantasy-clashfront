# ⚔️ Clash Front — Getting Started & How to Play

> *Use Your Strength to Change the World.*
>
> Clash Front is a persistent browser war game on the real hexagon-city NFT land map. The world
> never resets. Every battle changes the map. This guide covers **the MVP you can play today**,
> then the objectives, goals and rewards of the full game it grows into.

---

## 1. What is Clash Front?

Think **Romance of the Three Kingdoms** (territory, generals, logistics) meets **League of
Legends** (you personally drop into battles) on a **persistent world map** where land is real —
every parcel is an NFT someone can own. You are a warlord: claim land, develop it, raise and
provision armies, march them in real time, and fight for every parcel.

---

## 2. Quickstart (MVP — 2 minutes to war)

1. **Enter** — open the game URL, type a name. That's it: you get **2,000 CT** and your officer
   roster: your **Hero** (that's *you*) plus **Masters** — the generals you command.
2. **Read the map** — colored parcels are owned (each color = a player/NPC kingdom). Dark
   parcels are **wild**; a red eye means monsters live there. Your parcels glow with your color.
3. **Claim your first parcel** — click a wild parcel **without monsters** → *Claim*. One of your
   officers is assigned as overseer (every territory you hold needs one — officers are your
   occupation limit!).
4. **Raise an army** — click your parcel → *Raise Army*. A STANDARD army (100 infantry / 60
   archers / 40 cavalry) costs **~800 CT total**: 620 for troops + 180 in **provisions** — food
   🍞, gold 🪙, wood 🪵 it carries. Your 2,000 CT = two armies. Spend wisely.
5. **March** — click your army, then a destination. You'll see: path length, **arrival time**
   (marching takes real minutes — distance is a weapon), a **strength preview** vs the defenders,
   how much **food** the trip+battle needs, and what **command-center** tier your gold+wood
   affords. Confirm to march. Everyone can see armies marching.
6. **Battle** — when your army reaches hostiles, the battle resolves and the parcel **catches
   fire** 🔥 on the map. Strength, morale, food, and your command center decide it — your officer
   helps, but never more than 20%.
7. **Win → choose**: **PILLAGE** (instant CT loot, the land burns and loses value) or **OCCUPY**
   (the parcel becomes yours — needs a free officer). Raiders pillage; empire-builders occupy.
8. **Watch out** — evenly-matched battles end in a **STALEMATE** (gray smoke): nobody wins, and
   the attacker must **retreat**. If your army has no friendly parcel to retreat to, it
   **scatters** 💀. Plan your retreat line *before* you invade.

**You are not alone**: other players fight on the same map in real time, and NPC kingdoms (like
the Gnoll Dominion) expand on their own. The world moves whether you're online or not.

## 3. The core loop

```
 earn CT  →  claim/occupy land  →  raise & provision armies  →  march (real time)
    ↑                                                                  ↓
 pillage loot / territory yield   ←   PILLAGE or OCCUPY   ←   battle (fire on the map)
```

## 4. Objectives & goals

**Session goal (today):** expand your color. Clear monsters off the frontier, out-position other
players, and choose your identity:

- 🏴 **Raider** — pillage rich targets, live off loot, own little, fear nothing.
- 🏰 **Empire builder** — occupy, assign officers, grow a connected realm (adjacent parcels are
  easier to defend and give your armies safe retreat lines).
- ⚔️ **Warlord-for-hire** — hunt other players' armies; every march is visible, every avenue is
  an ambush point (streets funnel armies — hold the chokepoints).

**Campaign goals (as systems unlock):** control regions, siege estates (multi-battle campaigns
ending at a real castle), starve enemies by cutting supply lines, and shape the politics of a
Belgium-sized persistent world with 292,766 real parcels.

**The one rule that makes it strategy, not slugfest:** armies, food, morale and position decide
≥80% of every battle. Personal skill (drop-in hero play) swings at most 20%. Out-*think*, then
out-fight.

## 5. Rewards

**In the MVP (demo economy):**
| Action | Reward |
|---|---|
| Pillage a parcel | Instant CT loot (bigger for developed/populated land) |
| Occupy a parcel | Ongoing territory yield + a forward base + retreat point |
| Defeat armies | Weakens rivals permanently — casualties don't respawn |
| Expansion | More territory = more production = bigger armies |

**In the full game:**
- **CT is real and cross-game** — earned in EF Mobile / EF Hunt / EF MOBA, spent here on
  development, troops, and provisions. Every war burns CT; every victory earns it back with land.
- **Land NFTs** — parcels are ownable assets. **Landlords** earn a tax share when their land
  flourishes (whoever governs it); prosperous land = real yield. Estates (up to ~6,800 acres)
  are the crown jewels, with hand-designed castles.
- **Masters** — rentable general NFTs; your roster of up to 52 caps how much land you can
  oversee (55 with your 3 Heroes). Generals get KO'd; revives are limited. Fame follows victory.
- **Pets** — ownable/capturable NFT companions that **farm and guard** your land (they can be
  beaten in a raid but never lost). Defense you can't lose; offense stays scarce.
- **Contracts & politics** — mercenary jobs, bounties on enemy heroes, alliances, vassals,
  tribute. Reputation is a currency too.

## 6. Ten tips from the war college

1. Two cheap armies beat one expensive one — you can't be in two places with one banner.
2. **Ties favor the defender.** Never attack even; bring 1.5× strength or don't march.
3. Food is the clock: an underfed army fights weak and *must* win fast.
4. Gold+wood = your command center. "None ⚠" on the preview means you siege with no camp — don't.
5. Claim parcels **adjacent** to yours: connected land = retreat lines = no scattering.
6. Watch the streets. Armies funnel through avenues — good places to hunt, bad places to be slow.
7. Monsters get stronger away from the center. Level your ambitions outward.
8. Officers are your real cap. An officer leading an army isn't overseeing land — expansion vs
   governance is THE trade-off.
9. The map never lies: every march is visible. If you can see them, they can see you.
10. Pillage what you can't hold. Occupy what you can defend. Never do neither.
