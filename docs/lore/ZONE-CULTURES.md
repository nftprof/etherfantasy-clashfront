# ZONE CULTURES — twelve peoples, not twelve biomes

> **v1, 2026-07-10 (Fable 5).** The culture cards from the depth review (Part I #4): a greeting, a
> festival (hooks the `docs/12` seasonal calendar), a dish (from signature materials), an architecture
> motif, a proverb, a town-naming convention, and the named wild clans. Machine mirror:
> `data/zone-cultures.json`. Masters named only where the homes pass placed them (fe-website
> `masterHomes.json`). Ages referenced are `WORLD-CHRONICLE.md`.

---

## TIANXIA — the Capital Heartland *(surface · the destination)*
**Greeting:** "All roads." (reply: "And all of them here.") · **Festival:** **the Convergence** — the
day the year's newcomers arrive through the ports; lanterns strung along every road into the capital.
**Dish:** crossroads hotpot — one pot, whatever every traveler brought. **Architecture:** concentric
ring-walls and gate-arches; every building faces a road. **Proverb:** *"The center holds whoever holds
the center."* **Towns:** named for roads and gates (Ninth Gate, Long Road, Two Fords). **Clans:** the
Free Caravans (neutral), the Drill-Yards (combat schools). **Of note:** somewhere in the world's biggest
crowd is **Waldo** — finding him is a festival game older than the walls.

## MYTHORIA — the Western Carnival Coast *(surface · starts US West N / Montréal S)*
**Greeting:** "Enjoy the show." · **Festival:** **Maskfall** — the carnival's anniversary; five great
masks paraded, none worn after sundown (the Second Age is why). **Dish:** blossom-honey skewers and
festival fish. **Architecture:** boardwalks, lantern-piers, pavilion tents that never quite come down.
**Proverb:** *"Every prize on this shore was paid for twice."* **Towns:** named like attractions (Last
Tide, Wheelhouse, Petal Row). **Clans:** carnival spirits (leaf/insect kin), the Quiet Troupe (never
speaks, always watching). **Of note:** the sweet one, **Choco**, keeps a stall no one can find twice.

## PORTHAVEN — the Northern Commercial Coast *(surface · starts Singapore)*
**Greeting:** "What's it worth to you?" · **Festival:** **First Tide** — the year's first fleet out; the
Salt Gate opened with a coin thrown from every deck. **Dish:** twice-salted fish and delta oysters.
**Architecture:** warehouse-keeps on stilts, chained harbors, ledger-halls. **Proverb:** *"They salt
everything twice here, and trust no one who arrives by land."* **Towns:** named for cargo (Saltquay,
Copperwharf, Barrelrow). **Clans:** the Delta Toll-Gangs, the Customs Wraiths (toxin kin in the marsh).

## ARCADIA — the Academy Highlands *(surface · starts Tokyo · the current map)*
**Greeting:** "What did you learn?" · **Festival:** **the Examinations** — the rimwall lit with study
lamps; pass, and the Academy rings its one bell for you. **Dish:** highland tea and stone-oven rice.
**Architecture:** terraced cloisters, torii-arched passes, the Grand Academy's tiered roofs.
**Proverb:** *"The mountain does not move for the ignorant."* **Towns:** named for lessons (First
Question, Inkstone, Bellterrace). **Clans:** earth-kin quarry orders, telepath study-circles.

## AEROPOLIS — the Cloud Gateway Isle *(sky · ×2)*
**Greeting:** "Fair winds up." · **Festival:** **Mooring Day** — the anniversary of the first airship
mooring; every dock flies its first rope. **Dish:** cloud-orchard fruit, eaten falling-fresh.
**Architecture:** mesa terraces, mast-towers, rope-bridges with prayer flags. **Proverb:** *"Ascent is
earned; the fall is free."* **Towns:** named for winds (Updraft, First Gust, Longlift). **Clans:**
flyer-kin courier lodges, the Wind Shepherds.

## EMBERFALL — the Storm & Lava Isle *(sky · ×2)*
**Greeting:** "Still standing?" (reply: "Still burning.") · **Festival:** **the Quenching** — one night
a year the great forge is banked, and every smith drinks. **Dish:** ember-wine and ash-baked root.
**Architecture:** tiered fire-fortress, obsidian ramparts, chimney-spires. **Proverb:** *"Courage is
fuel."* **Towns:** named for forgework (Firstspark, Slagline, the Bellows). **Clans:** fire-kin forge
guilds, storm-callers on the caldera rim. **Of note:** **Dragon Cho** roosts the high crater — the
upper of the two dragon courts.

## EMPYREA — the High Sanctum Isle *(sky · ×2)*
**Greeting:** a bow; words are optional here. · **Festival:** **the Stillness** — one silent day; even
the wind is said to keep it. **Dish:** frost-herb broth, served without salt (want nothing). 
**Architecture:** white sanctums, glass-ice colonnades, bell-towers that ring themselves.
**Proverb:** *"The light asks nothing. Match it."* **Towns:** named for virtues (Patience, Clearwater,
Kept Word). **Clans:** ice-kin choristers, light-touched pilgrim orders. **Of note:** **Agena** — the
star-scholar — reads tomorrow from the sanctum's highest balcony.

## IRONHOLD — the Upper Caverns *(underworld · ×2.5)*
**Greeting:** two knocks on stone. · **Festival:** **Veinsday** — a new Midas seam struck; the whole
warren gets one free swing at it. **Dish:** fungal stew in bread you could pave with. **Architecture:**
pillared warrens, gate-vaults, rune-braced shafts. **Proverb:** *"The deeper the richer; the richer,
the less of you comes back."* **Towns:** named for depth (Firstdelve, Ninth Landing, Lampsend).
**Clans:** the kobold mining clans (each seam has one), iron-kin smith-holds.

## BLACKMERE — the Deep Caverns *(underworld · ×3.5)*
**Greeting:** "Eat before you go down." · **Festival:** **the Unfinished Feast** — a table set at the
lake edge for the Drowned Banquet's guests; no one sits, everyone leaves a dish. **Dish:** blind-fish
and black-lake salt (never taken in seconds — manners matter here). **Architecture:** causeway keeps,
drowned colonnades, the dead keep's silhouette on every coin. **Proverb:** *"Hunger is a door. Keep it
shut."* **Towns:** named for water (Stillmere, Lastlight Landing, the Shallows). **Clans:** phantom-kin
lantern-bearers, amphibious water-kin raft-folk.

## LUXURIA — the Inferno Vault *(underworld · ×5 · end-game)*
**Greeting:** "What do you want?" — never answered honestly. · **Festival:** **the Bloom** — one night
the Gardens flower all at once; picking one costs exactly what it's worth. **Dish:** garden fruit that
tastes like whatever you miss most. **Architecture:** twilight gardens, mirror-basalt courts, the Vault
itself. **Proverb:** *"Everything blooms here. Nothing bears fruit."* **Towns:** few, nameless by
custom — you don't put roots in Luxuria. **Clans:** dragon-kin hoard-courts, mystic covenants.
**Of note:** the deep court — **Kuman**, Luxuria's greatest treasure, gilded and coveted; **Cor** coiled
around the hoard (the lower dragon court); and **Blis**, who belongs to the deep and knows it.

## OLYMPUS — the Founders' Isle *(prestige · teleport-only)*
**Greeting:** "Welcome back." (everyone is presumed to have been before.) · **Festival:** **Foundation
Day** — the world's birthday, told in toasts. **Dish:** whatever the founders are having. 
**Architecture:** palm-crescent villas on made land. **Proverb:** *"Someone built all this. Sit down and
hear who."* **Towns:** one, itself. **Clans:** none — founders are people, not masters, and the isle is
theirs. Masterless by design.

## FORTUNA — the Influencers' Isle *(prestige · teleport-only)*
**Greeting:** "You look incredible." · **Festival:** **the Premiere** — always of something. **Dish:**
gold-leaf everything. **Architecture:** marina glamour, mirror pools, one very long red carpet.
**Proverb:** *"Be seen or be scenery."* **Towns:** one, and it photographs beautifully. **Clans:**
entourages. **Of note:** **Eldora** — El Dorado herself, the golden one — Fortuna's first and, she'd
note, therefore foremost resident.
