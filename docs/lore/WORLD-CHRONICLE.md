## THE FIRST AGE — the Age of the Weave (creation)

The world was not built. It was **wanted**.

In the beginning there was the **Desire Weave** — the raw current of longing from which all things here
are spun. And longing has weight:

- **Desire sinks.** The heavy wants — hunger, gold-lust, dominion, craving — pooled downward, and their
  weight **carved the caverns**: the underworld is not dug, it is *wanted into being*. Desire pooled by
  its kind: the gold-lust ran into the stone and became the **Midas veins** (why iron and gold run
  richest the deeper you mine — the depth gradient is the Weave's sediment). Hunger pooled into the
  flooded halls of the **Drowned Banquet**. Craving pooled deepest of all, into the twilight
  **Gardens of Enamora**.
- **Aspiration rises.** The light wants — hope, wonder, the wish to be better — rose from the world like
  heat, and where they gathered they **crystallized into aether**. The three sky isles are **frozen
  aspiration**: that is why they float, why aether is found nowhere below, and why no road leads up —
  *ascent must be earned* (the airship-gating and rare-gates are not rules; they are physics).
- **The surface is the balance.** Between the sinking and the rising lies the mortal world — the four
  continents where wanting is still a choice. **Tianxia sits at the world's center of gravity**, where
  the pulls cancel: *all under heaven*, and all roads lead there.

Where desire crystallizes instead of flowing, it hardens into **Covet Crystals** — stones that store a
wish and grant power at the cost of the wisher's own essence. (Gems, dark-essence, the deep rare nodes:
the economy's rarest materials **are** solidified desire, which is why they sit at the bottom of the
world and the top of the value ladder.)

## THE SECOND AGE — the Age of the Carnival (the Sundering)

A carnival came to the western coast, and it never entirely left.

It came in the age when the Weave ran strongest — a festival of lights on the shore of what is now
**Mythoria** (the *Carnival Coast* is not a theme; it is a **scar**). The carnival was a door. Its keeper
wore many faces — the chronicles name her **Yui** — and through its games the unwary were drawn down into
the realm their own wanting had built.

In that age **Kage no Mamoru**, the Shadow Warden, rose in the deep — lord of the **Bastion of Power on
the Isle of Dominus**, feeding on the pooled desire of ages. His reach cracked the surface at the world's
very center: the **SHAFT** that pierces Tianxia's heart is the wound where the deep first broke through.

Then came the **First Descent** — the story every tavern in the world still tells (and which the old
companion saga, the *EF Hunt*, let mortals live for themselves): **Jiro**, who went down for love, and
**Ayume**, who was taken and did not wait to be saved but fought her own way through the trials.

**The Descent has two rites, and every soul who goes below learns them:**

- **The Cut.** The deepest realm cannot be walked into. The only passage to the third deep is the
  **Blood Scimitar** — the blade without peer: one cut, shoulder to shoulder, painless, **the sacrifice
  you choose**. It does not kill you. It *takes* you — and you wake in Luxuria. (This is why no army has
  ever marched into the third deep, and why its gate-warden faces those who *chose* to come.)
- **The Bowl.** Every descender carries a **Rice Bowl** filled with mortal rice — the taste of the world
  above, and the anchor of their humanity. While the bowl holds, you remain human in a realm that is
  not. **Empty it — lose it — and you are human no longer:** the world above disowns you, the hunters
  come for you, and you become **one of the haunting instead of the haunted**. The Hollowed, they are
  called: they can never return above ground, and the phantom-kin that drift Blackmere's lantern-dark
  and the old graveyards are what became of them. *(Every Phantom in the world was once somebody's
  descent that ran out of rice.)*

Seven trials through the sin-domains; the **five Masks** of the carnival worn and set aside (Harlequin,
Plague Doctor, Lunar Moth, Siren, Phoenix — relics that survive to this day); and at the end the
**Sigil of Binding** — the knot-pattern seal by which the Shadow Warden was not slain but *bound*.

**The boss-gates of the underworld are the locks of that binding.** Ironhold's gate, Blackmere's gate,
the final vault of Luxuria — each seal holds a portion of the old dark, and each is guarded. This is why
the deep is gated, why its wardens are stronger than any mortal army (the ×2.5/×3.5/×5.0 bands), and why
what leaks past the seals — the wild, the bosses — must be beaten back over and over.

## THE THIRD AGE — the Age of Ashes (the Long Peace, ending now)

After the Descent, the world exhaled.

The old kingdoms that had fought in the Sundering crumbled — their keeps are the **ruins** that dot every
continent, their wars the **cairns** on the old battlefields. The **Masters** — the fifty-two great
generals of the age — hung up their banners and settled into the lands whose stories fit them (the homes
they keep today). The wilds crept back over the untended parcels. The aspirants built the **airship
ways** to the sky isles and the sanctums grew bright and strict above (*High Society* — the heavens keep
their own law, and it is not gentle to the unworthy). The prestige isles rose in the bay — **Olympus**
for the founders of the world, **Fortuna** for its brightest voices — reachable by no road, because they
were never meant to be marched upon.

And now the Long Peace ends. The seals are old. The gates weaken. The populace multiplies and the
granaries fill and the **warlords arrive** — you — to claim, to build, to march. From this day the
Chronicle no longer records the past: **it records what the players do** (see `WORLD-REMEMBERS-AND-TOWNS`
— great battles named forever, first deeds inscribed at the gates in the doers' names). The Fourth Age
is not written here, because the Fourth Age is the game.

## The Sky mirror (the HS half, authored per the owner's "opposite direction")

| Underworld (desire, sinking) | Sky (aspiration, rising) |
|---|---|
| **Ironhold** — Greed: the Midas veins, wealth hoarded in stone | **Aeropolis** — Hope: the Gateway, the first wind of *upward*; freedom earned |
| **Blackmere** — Gluttony & Dominion: the Drowned Banquet; the Bastion of Power's dead keep | **Emberfall** — Courage & Sacrifice: the forge that burns *for* something; the storm faced |
| **Luxuria** — Lust: the Gardens of Enamora, wanting that consumes | **Empyrea** — Grace: the Sanctum, wanting stilled; the light that asks nothing |

Same Weave, opposite current. The deep **takes** essence for power (Desire Weave's price); the heights
**ask** it freely given (why the sanctums are strict, why the sky wardens meet you at exactly your
strength ×2 — they test, they do not prey).
