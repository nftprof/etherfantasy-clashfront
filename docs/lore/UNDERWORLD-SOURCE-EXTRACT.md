## SOURCE: underworld world built and rules themes.docx

Synopsis: When souls from the mundane world are called into the Underworld—a realm sculpted by the very essence of human longing—they find themselves entangled in an Isekai ordeal shadowed by their deepest desires. Seven souls will traverse this dark fantasy landscape, each drawn into a twisted carnival of their own psyche's making, where the pursuit of love becomes a dangerous game against the backdrop of an ever-shifting domain that feeds on their innermost yearnings and fears.
Rules of Magic and Technology:
The Underworld operates under a mystical framework called "Desire Weave," where magic is a direct extension of the inhabitants' deepest wishes and darkest cravings.
Every soul can tap into the Desire Weave, but power comes with a cost—each spell cast siphons a fragment of the soul’s own life essence, creating a delicate balance between desire and self-preservation.
Technology from the mortal realm is twisted by the Underworld, operating erratically or gaining sentience. It often takes on a more organic and arcane demeanour, blending with the realm's magical essence.
Artifacts known as "Covet Crystals" can store desires and amplify magical abilities, but they can corrupt the user if overused, leading to an insatiable hunger for power.
Geography and Climate:
The Realm of Greed/Wealth is situated in the valley of Midas, a resplendent area where the opulence of structures overshadows the barrenness of its spiritless landscape.
The Domain of Gluttony thrives in the ever-expansive territory of Feastfall Plains, with climate shifting from pleasant to extreme as one's indulgence grows—leading to storms of unquenchable hunger.
The Bastion of Power rises on the Isle of Dominus, floating above a sea of mists. The climate there is arid and charged with static energy, reflecting the tense battles for supremacy.
The Gardens of Lust bloom in the heart of Enamora, an area veiled in perpetual twilight, where warmth and cool breezes alternate, mirroring the fleeting nature of desire.

Principle of Equivalent Desire:
In the Underworld, the magic system operates on the Principle of Equivalent Desire. The strength and efficacy of any magical act are directly proportional to the depth of genuine desire driving it. This principle demands a profound personal investment of will and intention, tying the caster's fate to the outcome of their magic.
Consequences of Overuse:
Utilising magic tied to one's desires is risky. Overuse of this power can result in the user being consumed by the Underworld, symbolically and literally. The realm will amplify and reflect their unbridled desires, transforming the character into a part of its fabric or birthing monstrous embodiments of their avarice.
Desire's Reflection:
The Underworld's landscape and the creatures that inhabit it mirror the desires and fears of those who enter. A character's internal struggles and aspirations can give birth to environments or entities that either aid or obstruct their journey, often serving as a test of their resolve and moral compass.
Magic of Binding and Contracts:
Magic in the Underworld can create potent bindings and contracts. Engagements sealed with blood or significant tokens create unbreakable pacts, which, if violated, can have dire consequences for the defaulting party, including curses or loss of magical abilities.
Limitation of Scope:
Magic cannot be wielded frivolously; it requires clarity of purpose and intent. Vague or unfocused desires produce weak or chaotic magic, which can backfire or wreak havoc on the user and their surroundings. The requirement for specificity means that magical acts are often ritualistic and deliberate.
Anchors of Humanity:
To prevent oneself from being consumed by magic and the Underworld, characters may use 'anchors'—items or memories from their life before the Underworld. These serve as reminders of their humanity and can stabilize their use of magic, providing protection against the corrupting influence of the realm.
Cost of Power:
Every use of magic exacts a toll on the user, appropriate to the power and scope of the spell. This might manifest as physical and emotional fatigue, temporary loss of senses or abilities, or an erosion of the soul if one delves too deeply into forbidden magics.
Magic's Influence on Society:
The society within the Underworld is hierarchically structured based on one's mastery and control over one's desires and, consequently, their magical prowess. Those who can wield powerful magic without succumbing to their desires are in positions of power and influence.
Forbidden Magic:
Certain magical practices, such as necromancy or curses that bind another’s free will, are considered taboo and forbidden, as they typically require the invocation and manipulation of desires against the natural order, leading to corruption and imbalance in the user.
Rule of Sacrifice:
True magical acts may require a sacrifice—a relinquishment of something valued. The greater the intended effect of the magic, the more significant the sacrifice must be. This forms a fundamental law of exchange, weighing the user's intentions against the equilibrium of the Underworld.
In essence, magic in the Underworld is a double-edged sword, offering immense potential while also presenting significant risk. Its use is an intricate dance with desire, where one false step can lead to being consumed by the very power they sought to wield. The characters must navigate this perilous terrain, balancing their innermost yearnings with the stark realities of a realm that reflects and magnifies the darkness within.

## SOURCE: Treatment.docx

Logline
After a seductive outsider named Yui tempts them into a treacherous magical world, young couple Jiro and Ayume have their love put to the test as Jiro delves into the darkness to rescue Ayume from the clutches of the evil Kage no Mamoru and his monstrous minions.

Synopsis:
This VR Isekai series comprises seven short, intense episodes transporting viewers alongside Jiro and Ayume from a magical carnival into a haunting underworld. With each 5-minute episode, the audience is drawn deeper into a rich tapestry of challenges and character growth, all while the threads of destiny, power, and connection weave a complex narrative designed to be experienced in an immersive VR landscape.

Characters:
Jiro: A young man with a zest for life and an unquenched curiosity, Jiro's encounter with a cryptic prophecy sets him on a path of adventure and self-discovery.
Ayume: Jiro's close friend and confidant, Ayume's initial trepidation about the unknown blossoms into courageous resolve as she faces the underworld's trials by Jiro's side.
Yui: A captivating and mysterious figure, Yui's playful and provocative nature belies a deeper, enigmatic agenda that intertwines her fate with Jiro and Ayume's.

ZONES

Realm of Greed/Wealth: Streets paved in gold and structures of precious stones define this glittering cityscape. Spirits are entranced by wealth, and the air is thick with the scent of minted gold, yet an eerie desolation pervades.

Domain of Gluttony: An endless banquet table offers a perpetual feast. Creatures gorge endlessly in an atmosphere of indulgence. Despite the abundance, an underlying sense of emptiness lingers.

Bastion of Power: Dominated by fortresses and arenas, this zone crackles with the energy of spells and the clash of weapons. It's a testament to the desire for control and dominance, with every interaction a power play.

Gardens of Lust: A seductive garden filled with sensuous fragrances and alluring beings. It's designed to tantalise and entice, playing on the deepest yearnings of the heart.

Episode 1: "Carnival"

Jiro and Ayume arrive at a bustling carnival as night descends. Their exploration of the lively fairgrounds brings them closer, culminating in a visit to a mysterious fortune teller. The teller's ominous prophecy about their future leaves them both intrigued and concerned, setting them on a path of unexpected adventure.

Episode 2: "Masquerade"

At the carnival, Jiro and Ayume delve deeper into its wonders, stumbling upon a magical puppet stall and an enchanting masquerade. Their night takes a dramatic turn when they are separated in the crowd, leading Jiro into an encounter with the mysterious Yui. This meeting sets them on a course toward a mystical altar and a guarded portal, hinting at greater mysteries ahead.

Episode 3: "The Rite of Blades"

In a mystical clearing, Jiro witnesses Ayume and Yui partake in a solemn and symbolic ritual, marking a pact with blood. Meanwhile, Jiro faces his destiny with a magical scimitar, enduring a transformative ordeal that imprints him with arcane sigils. The episode concludes with Jiro stepping through a portal, leaving behind the known world for an enigmatic new realm.

Episode 4: "Realm of the Diminutive"

Jiro's journey through a mystical portal transforms him into a miniature version of himself, leading to the discovery of a tiny door that opens into an otherworldly realm. In this new world, he encounters surreal gardens and a haunting river that reveals a chilling prophecy of loss and betrayal.

Episode 5: "Grove of Nightmares"

An ethereal dance in a grove quickly descends into a nightmare as grotesque creatures attack. Ayume is captured, leaving Jiro and Yui to seek refuge in a desolate structure. Here, Jiro confronts Yui, blaming her for their plight, before they are forced to face the grim reality of their situation.

Episode 6: "Echoes of the Past"
A quake in the Underworld separates Jiro from Yui, leading him to a confrontation with distorted memories and a changed Yui. They encounter Yume, who offers guidance and an enchanted compass to face the Kage no Mamoru in the Castle of Shadows, marking a crucial step in their journey.

Episode 7: “Kage no Mamoru”
Jiro and Yui's arrival at the Castle of Shadows brings battles with monstrous denizens, forcing Jiro to tap into his growing shadow powers. They rescue Ayume but at a cost to Jiro's humanity. The trio leaves the castle with Jiro grappling with the darkness within him

Themes and Elements:
The series weaves themes of destiny versus free will, the allure of forbidden power, and the strength found in companionship. The VR environment accentuates the sensory details of the carnival's gaiety and the underworld's eerie beauty, making the viewer a part of the unfolding drama.

Style and Tone:
"Beyond the Twilight Veil" oscillates between the exuberant, saturated colours of the carnival and the stark, shadowy contrasts of the underworld, with VR enabling an intimate exploration of these polarised worlds. The tone evolves from wonder and excitement to tension and introspection, mirroring the characters' emotional journeys.

Conclusion:
The series culminates in a confrontation with literal and metaphorical darkness, as Jiro's sacrifices and the characters' inner strengths lead to a precarious balance of loss and hope. The ending hints at continued struggles within and beyond the underworld, leaving viewers contemplating the true cost of power and the enduring light of human connection.

As evening falls, painting the sky in deep blues and purples, Jiro and Ayume are seated on the back of a rustic wagon along a cobblestone path. The carnival, bustling with activity and illuminated by many lights, promises an evening of wonder and excitement.

Episode 3: "The Rite of Blades"

Sinister Welcome:
Jiro steps into an otherworldly clearing shrouded in a dim, unsettling twilight. He calls out for Ayume and Yui, but his voice seems to dissolve into the thick, charged air. The two women are deeply engrossed in a solemn ritual, seemingly oblivious to his presence.

The blood from their wounds flows down, seeping into the fabric of the scarf and dripping onto the ground below, a vivid testament to their commitment. Grotesque dancers of the carnival encircle him, their bodies contorting in jerks and twitches, a macabre audience to his plight.

Compelled by an invisible force, the Scimitar rises from the stone, levitating in a halo of eldritch energy—Jiro, with a mixture of horror and determination, steps forward. The blade, as if guided by the will of the underworld itself, passes cleanly through him from shoulder to shoulder, a transcendent pain that leaves no wound but imprints him with a constellation of glowing, magical sigils. A black tattoo of the sword is imprinted on Jiro’s back. 

Transformation and Transition: Jiro's laugh, a crazy maniacal sound of shock mingled with newfound power.

The portal, activated by the ritual and the scimitar's enchantment, unveils a path steeped in shadow and uncertainty and Jiro steps through.

In the shadow-drenched throne room of the Underworld, treachery unfolds as Ayume betrays her allies Yui and Jiro, revealing her allegiance to the dark ruler Kage no Mamoru. As Ayume ascends the throne, Yui is struck down and absorbed into the onyx stone, while Jiro is ensnared in a blood ritual that binds him to Ayume's will, heralding a new era of darkness.

OVA2 Outline
Beat 1: The Throne of POWER (Power)
In an ominous, dimly lit throne room carved from onyx stone, deep within the Underworld, towering walls inscribed with ancient runes shimmer with malevolent energy. A grand throne stands at the room's heart, with a dark shadow seated upon it, exuding formidable power. T tense and ready for battle, Yui and Jiro enter the throne room. Ayume, her cloak trailing behind her, walks forward with a confident swagger, the soft sound of its hems brushing against the stone floor, signalling the beginning of a fateful confrontation.

Beat 2: The Gathering of Shadows
The spectral figure on the throne emerges as Kage no Mamoru, eliciting gasps from Yui and a clenched sword from Jiro, poised for combat. As Kage no Mamoru stands and bows before Ayume, she purposefully ascends the throne, sitting with a dark aura and eyes reflecting chilling resolve. Yui and Jiro watch in horror as Ayume's sinister smile reveals her true allegiance, unmasking her as the true orchestrator of their dark journey.

Beat 3: The Coronation of Betrayal
At Ayume's silent command, Kage no Mamoru approaches Yui, striking her with a burst of dark energy. Yui's cry is silenced as she collapses, her essence absorbed into the stone, cementing Ayume's rise to power. This moment marks the coronation of betrayal as Ayume assumes her dark throne with a chilling air of triumph.

Beat 4: The Birth of Darkness Within
The throne room becomes a chaotic vortex of shadows and echoing chants as the dark ritual climaxes. Jiro's anguished scream fills the air, his shadow contorting to mirror the inner turmoil and darkness consuming him. Ayume reveals the harsh truth to Jiro, now grappling with the realisation that he was merely a pawn in her grand scheme. Bound by the blood ritual, Jiro struggles against the dark forces tying his fate to Ayume's will, a tragic hero caught in a web of deceit and dark magic.
