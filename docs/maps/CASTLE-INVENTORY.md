## Notes
- **PALACE (3-ring)** = the top rank, on EPIC estates (plus story-designated palaces such as the Vault-Palace of Luxuria on a LARGE estate — EF Hunt EP7 finale).
- **`(no L3)`** in the castle-parcel column (16 castles) = the estate is not L3-subdivided; its battlefield is the **whole-estate map** — a committed flagship at `data/cf-maps/parcels/<estate>.json` where one exists, else the baked default (`worldfield.estateParcel`, the authored fort kept, stepping down a tier only if the sim gate refuses it).
- **Ladder forts** (the un-authored EPIC/GIANT/LARGE estates' PALACE/CASTLE/KEEP defaults, `MAP-POPULATION-GUIDE.md` §1) are NOT listed here — they live only in the server registry and auto-name themselves; the §6 B-4 naming pass promotes them into the fields (and so into this inventory).
- Every castle grows real WALL/GATE/TOWER ring geometry in the map generator — the rings ARE the land (map-lane), warden-court interiors are engine-lane.
- CGI (Olympus) and KOL (Fortuna) fields are authored but uncharted (no parcel geometry yet) — their castles have no view id until the zones are charted.
