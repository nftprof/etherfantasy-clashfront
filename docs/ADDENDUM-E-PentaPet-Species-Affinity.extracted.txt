Addendum E — PentaPet Species AffinityFrameworkDesign PhilosophyPentaPets are the foundation of the Ether Fantasy ecosystem.They are not merely collectible creatures or combat companions. They are the living population of theworld and unify every Ether Fantasy game mode.A single PentaPet may:Be discovered or captured in EF HuntBe trained and specialized within a kingdomWork as part of the kingdom economyJoin armies in Clash FrontFight in EF MOBAContinue progressing throughout its lifetimeThis creates one persistent creature ecosystem shared across every game.Design PrinciplesSpecies Define TalentSpecies determine natural aptitude.They do not permanently restrict occupations.Every PentaPet can perform every task, but each species naturally excels in different professions.This avoids "dead" species while creating meaningful economic specialization.Experience Defines MasterySpecies provides an initial advantage.Experience determines long-term excellence.Example:An Earth-type begins as an excellent miner. 3
