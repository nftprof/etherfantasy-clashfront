## Balance guardrails (do not break)

- Mastery bonus stays ≤ +5% total — flavor, not power.
- Legendary items strong but losable on death — keep risk/reward.
- Player and enemy hero must stay roughly even in stats over a match.
- Keep the game LIGHT: one file, vanilla Three.js r128, no new libraries, no build step.
- Validate every change: serve over HTTP, load in browser, watch F12 console.
